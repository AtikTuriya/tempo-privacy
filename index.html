TEMPO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

TEMPO is a workout interval timer, strength log and run tracker for Android.

This policy describes what happens to your information. The short version: TEMPO has no account, no server and no analytics. Your workouts, plans, measurements and runs are stored on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

Two parts of the app talk to the internet, and neither carries anything about your training: advertising, and the Google Play rating dialog, which TEMPO asks Play to show and is told nothing about. Both are described below.

What stays on your phone

All of the following is written to storage on your device and nowhere else. There is no copy of it held by us, because there is no server to hold it on.

  • Workout plans, and your history of completed workouts
  • Logged sets — exercise, weight, reps
  • Body measurements you enter, such as weight
  • Finished runs — distance, duration, estimated calories, and the run's shape (see Location below; a shape, never a place)
  • Playlists, stored as references to audio files already on your phone
  • Settings: theme, units, weekly goal, cue preferences, reminders
  • Which achievements you have earned and which guided tutorials you have finished

Uninstalling TEMPO deletes all of it. Because none of it ever leaves the phone, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no copy to request.

Location

TEMPO uses your location for one purpose: to measure distance and pace while a run is actually running.

  • Your route is never saved. There is no field in the app's storage capable of holding a coordinate. TEMPO cannot show you where you ran, and neither can anyone who takes your phone.
  • A finished run does keep its shape. So that you can see a run back, TEMPO stores the pace of each stretch and how far it turned — how far east and north each part of the run moved from the part before it. It never stores where any of it was, including where you started. The drawing you see on a finished run is a shape floating on its own; it cannot be put on a map, by us or by anybody else.
  • Your position itself is used live and discarded, the moment the next reading arrives. It is never written to storage and never transmitted.
  • TEMPO uses a foreground service so a run keeps measuring with the screen off. This is why a notification appears while a run is in progress: so the app can never be measuring your location without telling you.
  • TEMPO does not request the "all the time" background location permission.

Location access can be revoked at any time in Android's settings. Runs stop working; nothing else does.

Music

If you build a playlist, TEMPO reads the audio files already on your device to list them. It stores references to those tracks and their titles, locally.

  • Audio only. TEMPO does not request access to photos or video.
  • No audio file, and no information about your music, is uploaded anywhere.

Advertising

TEMPO shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of most screens, and an optional video ad that you choose to watch to unlock some features for 24 hours. A video ad only ever starts when you tap to watch it. This is the only way information about your use of TEMPO reaches a third party.

To show ads, Google receives data such as your device's advertising identifier, device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads. That data is handled by Google, not by us — we never receive it, and we cannot see it. Google's practices are described in their own policy:

  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, TEMPO shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and no ad request is made unless you allow it. You can change that decision at any time from Settings.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings, under Privacy › Ads.

The free trial and the 24-hour unlock

New installs get a 14-day trial of the locked features. To run it, TEMPO keeps on your phone the date you first opened the app, the most recent day it counted, and how many days you have used. Watching a video ad unlocks the locked features for 24 hours; TEMPO keeps on your phone when that ends. Both records stay on the phone, are never sent anywhere by TEMPO, and are never in a backup you export.

Backups

Settings › Backup writes everything TEMPO holds into a single file and hands it to Android's share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice — an email to yourself, a cloud drive, a folder on the phone.

TEMPO does not upload it and does not know where it went. Once it leaves the app, the file is covered by whatever service you sent it to, not by this policy.

Automatic backup (optional, off by default)

If you want, Settings › Backup & export › Automatic backup lets you choose one folder. After that, each time you save a workout, a run, a measurement or a plan, TEMPO writes its backup file into that folder. It keeps two files and alternates between them, so a failed write cannot destroy your last good copy.

  • Nothing happens until you choose a folder, and you can turn it off at any time. Turning it off leaves the files already in the folder where they are.
  • It uses no extra Android permission. The folder is granted through Android's own folder picker, for that one folder, and Android lets you revoke it from its settings.
  • TEMPO runs nothing in the background for this. The file is written only while the app is open, just after something is saved.
  • If the folder you choose syncs to a cloud drive (Google Drive, OneDrive and so on), that service receives the file, under its own terms. TEMPO only writes into the folder; it does not upload anything and does not know whether the folder is synced.

The file holds the same things as a manual backup: your plans, workouts, sets, measurements, runs, playlists and settings. It never contains where you ran — that is not stored anywhere in TEMPO.

Notifications

Reminders you create on the Schedule screen are scheduled on your device by Android. Nothing is pushed to you from a server, because there is no server.

If you switch on "Warn me before I lose my streak", TEMPO works out which day this week your weekly goal stops being reachable and schedules one notification for that afternoon. That calculation happens entirely on your phone, from the workouts and runs already stored there. It is off unless you turn it on, and it never sends more than one notification a week.

Sending feedback

Settings → Send feedback opens your own email app with a message already drafted. TEMPO sends nothing: the draft sits in your mail app until you press send, and you can read and change every line first — including deleting the details it filled in.

Those details are the app's version and build number, your phone's maker and model, and its Android version. That is the whole list. It does not include anything about your training, and it does not include any identifier that is unique to your phone.

Rating TEMPO

After you have finished a few workouts, TEMPO may ask Google Play to show its own rating dialog. The dialog belongs to Play, not to TEMPO — we ask once, Google decides whether to show it, and we are told nothing in return, not even whether you saw it or what you said.

TEMPO never asks how you feel first and never routes people to different places based on the answer. There is one "Send feedback" row in Settings for anyone who wants it, and it is not offered as an alternative to leaving a review.

Children

TEMPO is not directed at children. Ad content is limited to a general-audience rating, and TEMPO does not tag ad requests as child-directed. If you believe a child has provided information through TEMPO, note that the app has no account system and stores nothing off the device — uninstalling removes everything.

Changes to this policy

If TEMPO starts doing something this policy does not describe, this policy changes first. The date at the top says when it last did.
